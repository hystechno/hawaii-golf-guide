## Twilight Rates: Golf's Best-Kept Discount

Most Hawaiian courses offer significantly reduced "twilight" rates for afternoon tee times. The exact hours vary, but typically start 2-4 hours before sunset.

### Typical Twilight Savings

| Course Type | Regular Rate | Twilight Rate | Savings |
|-------------|-------------|---------------|---------|
| Luxury Resort | $400 | $250 | $150 |
| Mid-Range Resort | $200 | $120 | $80 |
| Municipal | $75 | $50 | $25 |

### Twilight Strategy Tips

**Check sunset times** – Hawaii's sunset varies from 5:45 PM (winter) to 7:15 PM (summer). In summer, you can easily finish 18 holes with a 3:00 PM tee time.

**Consider 9-hole twilight rounds** – If time is tight, many courses offer even steeper discounts for 9-hole twilight play.

**Book in advance** – Popular courses sell out their limited twilight slots quickly.

**Bring a flashlight** – If you're slow or lose balls, that Hawaiian sunset comes fast once it starts.

## Golf Package Deals: Bundle and Save

Many resorts and hotels offer golf packages that include accommodations and green fees at significant discounts.

### Types of Golf Packages

**Stay-and-Play Packages**
- Combine hotel stays with golf at partner courses
- Often include complimentary range balls
- Typical savings: 15-25% off à la carte pricing

**Multi-Round Packages**
- Book 3-5 rounds at the same resort for bulk pricing
- Some include replay rates (same day second round)
- Typical savings: 20-30% per round

**Island Hopper Packages**
- Play multiple courses across different islands
- Often includes inter-island flights or ferries
- Best for extended Hawaii vacations

### Where to Find Package Deals

1. **Directly through resorts** – Call the golf shop, not just the hotel reservations
2. **Golf booking platforms** – GolfNow, TeeOff, and Supreme Golf often have package options
3. **Local golf wholesalers** – Companies like Hawaii Tee Times specialize in bundled deals

## Equipment: Rent vs. Bring Your Own

One of the biggest decisions for traveling golfers is whether to bring clubs or rent locally. Here's how to decide:

### When to Rent Clubs

**Rent if:**
- You're playing 1-2 rounds total
- Your home airline charges bag fees ($50-75 each way)
- You want to try premium clubs without commitment
- You're traveling with family and luggage space is tight

**Rental Costs:**
- Municipal courses: $30-50 per round
- Resort courses: $50-85 per round
- Premium sets (Titleist, Callaway): $75-100 per round

### When to Bring Your Own

**Bring clubs if:**
- You're playing 3+ rounds
- You have specific clubs you're comfortable with
- You have airline status with free checked bags
- You're particular about grip size, shaft flex, etc.

**Pro Money-Saving Tip:** Many courses offer "rental packages" for multiple days at discounted rates. A 3-day rental often costs only slightly more than single-day rates.

## Additional Money-Saving Strategies

### 1. Walk Instead of Ride

Many Hawaiian courses offer "walking rates" that save $15-25 per round compared to cart-included pricing. Plus:
- Hawaii's weather is perfect for walking
- You'll see more of the course's beauty
- Great exercise between mai tais

**Best Walking Courses:**
- Ala Wai (Waikiki)
- Pali Golf Course (Oahu)
- Wailua Golf Course (Kauai)

### 2. Book Direct with Courses

Third-party booking sites charge convenience fees (often $5-10 per booking). Call the course directly:
- Avoid booking fees
- Access unpublished resident/visitor rates
- Ask about unadvertised specials

### 3. Play During "Shoulder Season"

Hawaii's shoulder seasons (April-May and September-October) offer:
- Lower hotel rates
- Less crowded courses
- Occasional golf specials
- Same perfect weather

### 4. Join Course Email Lists

Many Hawaiian courses send exclusive offers to email subscribers:
- Last-minute tee time deals
- Weather-related "mud rates"
- Holiday specials
- Birthday discounts

### 5. Military and Kama'aina Discounts

If you qualify:
- Military ID holders often save 20-40%
- Hawaii residents (kama'aina) get significant breaks
- Some courses extend discounts to military retirees

### 6. Golf Card Programs

Several discount programs cover Hawaiian courses:
- **GolfPass** – Monthly subscription with waived fees and discounts
- **GolfNow Rewards** – Points program for frequent bookers
- **Local golf associations** – Join Oahu/Maui golf associations for member rates

## The Hidden Costs to Watch

Even "cheap" Hawaii golf has potential add-ons:

**Caddies/Forecaddies:** Some resort courses require them ($40-60 per bag)
**Range Balls:** $10-20 per bucket at premium courses
**Food and Beverage:** Resort courses markup heavily ($8 beers, $18 sandwiches)
**Tips:** Caddies, bag attendants, and cart staff expect gratuities

**Budget Tip:** Play morning rounds and bring your own snacks/water to minimize on-course spending.
