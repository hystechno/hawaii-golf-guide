## Introduction

Oahu has 37 golf courses spread across six regions, from a $43 municipal layout on the North Shore to $342 resort tee times at Turtle Bay. About two-thirds welcome visitors; the rest are private clubs or military courses with access rules. Public green fees for visitors run $35–$275.

That range is why Oahu is Hawaii's best golf island for most travelers: you can walk off a Waikiki beach onto the busiest municipal course in America, play a former LPGA venue, and tee it up on a PGA Tour host's doorstep — all in the same week. Below is the complete island directory, organized by region, with current visitor rates for every course (verified September 2026 — always confirm when you book, as several courses use dynamic pricing). When you've picked your courses, [compare tee times on golfscape](https://golfscape.com/oahu-golf-courses) to find the best rate for your dates.

## Honolulu & Waikiki

**[Ala Wai Golf Course](/courses/oahu-001/)** — Municipal · Par 70, 5,861 yds · $86 non-resident, cart $26
Minutes from Waikiki along the canal, with Diamond Head views. Flat, walkable, and famously busy — book early. Driving range open until 11 PM. Note: no twilight play until further notice.

**[Hawaii Kai Golf Course](/courses/oahu-007/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,500 yds · Championship $160–$170; Executive $50–$65
Two courses 13 miles east of Waikiki: a William Bell championship layout with real elevation change, plus a budget-friendly executive course.

**[Moanalua Golf Club](/courses/oahu-020/)** — Semi-private · 9 holes, Par 36 · $35 public play
Hawaii's oldest golf course (1898), designed by Scottish architect Donald MacIntyre in Moanalua Valley. A genuine piece of golf history at a municipal price.

**[Honolulu Country Club](/courses/oahu-011/)** — Private · Par 72, 6,564 yds
Arnold Palmer-designed members' club in Nuuanu. Visitor play requires a member connection.

**[Oahu Country Club](/courses/oahu-022/)** — Private · Par 71, 6,041 yds
Founded 1906 in Nuuanu Valley — old-Hawaii tradition, members only.

**[Waialae Country Club](/courses/oahu-031/)** — Private · Par 72, 7,020 yds
Host of the PGA Tour's Sony Open in Hawaii every January. The only way on as a visitor is with a member — or volunteer at the tournament.

### Military courses near Honolulu (access rules apply)

**[Ke'alohi Golf Course (Hickam)](/courses/oahu-009-a/)** — Military · 9 holes, Par 27 · $8–$12 for eligible players
Short layout on Joint Base Pearl Harbor-Hickam; base access required.

**[Mamala Bay Golf Course (Hickam)](/courses/oahu-009-b/)** — Military · Par 72, 6,868 yds · Guests $46
Ocean-view holes near the flight line; eligible guests can play with a sponsor.

**[Navy Marine Golf Course](/courses/oahu-021-b/)** — Military · Par 72, 6,754 yds · Civilian guests $52
Popular Pearl Harbor-area course; civilian guest play available.

**[Walter J. Nagorski Golf Course (Army)](/courses/oahu-030-b/)** — Military (public access) · Par 34, 2,837 yds · Public $38
Fort Shafter's compact layout near downtown — one of the easiest military courses for civilians to play.

## Windward Oahu (Kailua, Kaneohe & Waimanalo)

**[Pali Golf Course](/courses/oahu-024/)** — Municipal · Par 72, 6,605 yds · $86 non-resident, cart $26
Hillside muni in the Ko'olau foothills — the most dramatic scenery in the city system, with elevation changes and frequent rainbows. Bring your slope-adjusted yardages; a [Bushnell Tour V5 rangefinder](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B088X3MJHF?tag=hawaiigolfg02-20) earns its keep here.

**[Olomana Golf Links](/courses/oahu-023/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,306 yds · $95–$105 (domestic visitors)
Links-style and windy, beneath Mount Olomana. A locals' favorite that visiting golfers consistently underrate.

**[Royal Hawaiian Golf Club](/courses/oahu-026/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,609 yds · $200
Jungle golf between Mount Olomana and the Ko'olau range — first co-designed by Peter and Perry Dye, recently refreshed by Greg Norman. The most visually striking public course on the island.

**[Bay View Golf Park](/courses/oahu-002/)** — Public · Par 60, 3,399 yds · $51–$66 non-resident (walk/ride)
Semi-executive windward course with Kaneohe Bay views — a fun, fast round for a beach-day afternoon.

**[Mid-Pacific Country Club](/courses/oahu-018/)** — Private · Par 72, 6,792 yds
Premier members' club in Kailua.

**[Kaneohe Klipper Golf Course](/courses/oahu-013/)** — Military · Par 72, 6,528 yds · Civilian guests $55
Marine Corps Base Hawaii course with spectacular bayfront holes — routinely called the best military golf course in the world.

**[Ko'olau Golf Club](/courses/oahu-016/)** — Permanently closed September 30, 2020
The dramatic target-golf layout beneath the Ko'olau cliffs is gone; the listing remains for the many golfers still searching for it.

## Leeward Oahu & Ko Olina (Ewa Beach, Kapolei)

**[Ko Olina Golf Club](/courses/oahu-015/)** — Resort · Par 72, 6,815 yds · $245 resort guests / $275 visitors
Ted Robinson design and former LPGA Lotte Championship host, with water features everywhere and immaculate service. Free shuttle from Waikiki — a rare perk. Twilight drops to $210 after 1 PM. [Compare tee times on golfscape](https://golfscape.com/oahu-golf-courses) before booking direct.

**[Kapolei Golf Club](/courses/oahu-014/)** — Public · Par 72, 7,001 yds · $210; twilight $155 after 1:30 PM
Former LPGA Tour venue (2013–2018) with Bermuda fairways and bent-grass greens — arguably the best-conditioned daily-fee course on Oahu.

**[Hawaii Prince Golf Club](/courses/oahu-008/)** — Resort · 27 holes · $179 including cart and range balls
Arnold Palmer/Ed Seay design with three interchangeable nines and the best value among Oahu's resort-tier courses. Weekend twilight from 2:30 PM drops to $115 with a cart.

**[Coral Creek Golf Course](/courses/oahu-003/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,808 yds · $125 morning; $65 after 3 PM
Robin Nelson design in Ewa Beach with waterfall features; the tiered afternoon pricing is one of the island's best deals.

**[Royal Kunia Country Club](/courses/oahu-027/)** — Public · Par 72, 7,007 yds · $180 morning; $96 twilight
Target-style golf with big elevation changes and panoramic Pearl Harbor views.

**[360° Ewa Beach Country Club](/courses/oahu-004/)** — Semi-private · Par 72, 6,711 yds · $195; twilight $145
The former Ewa Beach Golf Club, reborn as a links-style Robin Nelson layout now open to visiting golfers.

**[West Loch Golf Course](/courses/oahu-033/)** — Municipal · Par 72, 6,335 yds · $86 non-resident, cart $26
Short on yardage, long on water hazards — the toughest-scoring course in the city system relative to its length.

**[Ewa Villages Golf Course](/courses/oahu-005/)** — Municipal · Par 73, 6,455 yds · $86 non-resident, cart $26
Links-style muni where the afternoon trade wind is the real defense. Accuracy beats distance here.

**[Hoakalei Country Club](/courses/oahu-010/)** — Private · Par 72, 7,413 yds
Long, modern members' course anchoring the Hoakalei resort community.

**[Barbers Point Golf Course (Navy)](/courses/oahu-021-a/)** — Military · Par 72, 6,679 yds · Civilian guests $46
Navy course at Kalaeloa with guest play available.

## Central Oahu (Mililani, Wahiawa & Waipahu)

**[Mililani Golf Club](/courses/oahu-019/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,455 yds · $160–$170; late twilight $90
Mountain-view course through central Oahu's cool uplands. The $90 late twilight is a steal; walking saves another $10.

**[Waikele Golf Club](/courses/oahu-032/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,663 yds · $195; early twilight $145
Water hazards guard nearly every hole on this popular layout next to the Waikele outlets.

**[Pearl at Kalauao](/courses/oahu-025/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,400 yds · $140; $95 after 1 PM
The transformed former Pearl Country Club — upgraded facilities, mountain-and-harbor views, and a pitch-and-putt loop included with your green fee.

**[Ted Makalena Golf Course](/courses/oahu-028/)** — Municipal · Par 71, 5,976 yds · $86 non-resident, cart $26
Pearl Harbor peninsula muni — flat, friendly, and a reliable last-minute tee time.

**[Hawaii Country Club](/courses/oahu-006/)** — Public · Par 71, 5,916 yds · $38–$48, cart $20
Recently renovated Wahiawa course under new management — among the cheapest full-length golf on the island.

**[Leilehua Golf Course](/courses/oahu-030-a/)** — Military/Public · Par 72, 7,019 yds · Public $47
Schofield Barracks course that welcomes civilian play — long layout, genuine bargain.

## North Shore

**[Arnold Palmer Course at Turtle Bay](/courses/oahu-029-a/)** — Resort · Par 72, 7,218 yds · $285–$342
Oahu's premier resort test, winding through wetlands and along the ocean at Turtle Bay Resort. Early twilight (after 1 PM) is $225.

**[Fazio Course at Turtle Bay](/courses/oahu-029-b/)** — Resort · Temporarily closed
The George Fazio layout is not currently operating; the Palmer course is Turtle Bay's only game right now.

**[Kahuku Golf Course](/courses/oahu-012/)** — Municipal · 9 holes, Par 70 · $43 non-resident
The closest thing to seaside links golf in Hawaii — a walking-only, ocean-breeze muni where $43 buys 18 holes (two loops) steps from the sand.

## Which Oahu Course Should You Play?

- **Best overall resort experience:** [Ko Olina](/courses/oahu-015/) — service, conditioning, and the free Waikiki shuttle.
- **Best pure golf test:** [Arnold Palmer Course at Turtle Bay](/courses/oahu-029-a/) — 7,218 yards of North Shore wind and wetlands.
- **Best-conditioned daily-fee:** [Kapolei](/courses/oahu-014/) — former LPGA venue with bent-grass greens.
- **Best scenery per dollar:** [Pali](/courses/oahu-024/) — Ko'olau cliffs and rainbows for a municipal $86.
- **Best budget round:** [Kahuku](/courses/oahu-012/) ($43, seaside links feel) or [Hawaii Country Club](/courses/oahu-006/) ($38–$48, recently renovated).
- **Best for a quick/short round:** [Bay View Golf Park](/courses/oahu-002/) (par 60 with bay views) or [Hawaii Kai's executive course](/courses/oahu-007/).
- **Best history:** [Moanalua](/courses/oahu-020/) — Hawaii's oldest course, $35.
- **Best-kept secret:** [Olomana](/courses/oahu-023/) — links-style windward golf most visitors never find.

## Practical Tips for Playing Oahu

1. **Book municipal tee times the moment the window opens.** Honolulu's city courses (Ala Wai, Pali, West Loch, Ewa Villages, Ted Makalena) are $86 for visitors — the demand matches the value.
2. **Afternoons are cheaper and windier.** Nearly every Oahu course discounts twilight play — Kapolei drops $55, Hawaii Prince nearly $65, Coral Creek down to $65 after 3 PM. Trade winds build after lunch, so club up.
3. **Pack extra gloves.** Humidity and sudden showers shred them — bring two or three, like the humidity-durable [FootJoy WeatherSof](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CCK9CRGX?tag=hawaiigolfg02-20), and rotate between nines.
4. **Bring wind-friendly balls.** Lava rock, ravines, and 20 mph trades eat golf balls; a penetrating ball like the [Titleist Pro V1](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DPN7QZ9R?tag=hawaiigolfg02-20) is the local standard. Buy in bulk locally if you run short.
5. **Rental cars beat rideshares for golf.** Courses are 20–50 minutes apart; Ko Olina's free Waikiki shuttle is the exception, not the rule.
6. **Renting vs. bringing clubs:** premium rental sets run $75–$95 at resort courses here. If you're playing four-plus rounds, shipping your own set ahead with [Ship Sticks](https://www.shipsticks.com) or picking up a pre-owned set from [2nd Swing](https://www.2ndswing.com) can pencil out better — our [Hawaii golf packing list](/blog/hawaii-golf-packing-list/) runs the full math.
7. **Kama'aina, bring your Hawaii ID.** Resident rates are dramatic: $28 weekday at city courses versus $86 visitor pricing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many golf courses are on Oahu?**
Oahu has 37 golf course listings: six municipal, 14 public or daily-fee, three operating resort courses, seven military, and five private clubs — plus two not currently operating (Ko'olau permanently, Turtle Bay's Fazio Course temporarily). Roughly two dozen are realistically playable for visitors.

**What is the best public golf course on Oahu?**
For conditions, Kapolei Golf Club ($210, former LPGA venue) leads the daily-fee pack, with Royal Hawaiian ($200) the most scenic. For value, the city municipal courses at $86 — especially Pali for scenery and Ala Wai for location — are hard to beat anywhere in America.

**How much does it cost to golf on Oahu?**
Visitor green fees range from $35 (Moanalua, 9 holes) and $43 (Kahuku) up to $342 at Turtle Bay's Palmer Course. Most quality public golf falls between $95 and $210, and twilight rates typically save 25–40%.

**Can visitors play military golf courses on Oahu?**
Some, with restrictions. Leilehua (Schofield Barracks) and Nagorski (Fort Shafter) openly welcome civilian play at $47 and $38. Kaneohe Klipper, Mamala Bay, Barbers Point, and Navy Marine allow civilian guests — typically with a military sponsor or base access. Always call ahead; policies shift with base security levels.

**Do you need a car to golf on Oahu?**
Strongly recommended. Only Ala Wai is walkable from Waikiki hotels. Ko Olina runs a free shuttle from Waikiki; for everything else — Turtle Bay is about an hour's drive — a rental car is the practical choice.

**What is the oldest golf course on Oahu?**
Moanalua Golf Club, built in 1898 in Moanalua Valley — the oldest course in Hawaii and one of the oldest west of the Rockies. It's a 9-hole, par-36 layout open to the public for $35.
