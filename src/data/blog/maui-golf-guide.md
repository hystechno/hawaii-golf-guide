## Introduction

Maui has 15 golf courses, and visitors can realistically play ten of them — headlined by the Plantation Course at Kapalua ($475), three Wailea resort layouts ($265–$325), and Ka'anapali's two classic designs. Value picks include Waiehu ($66–$86), Maui Nui ($99–$109), and Pukalani (from $49).

The Valley Isle packs more famous golf per square mile than anywhere in Hawaii: a PGA Tour host, former LPGA venues, and Robert Trent Jones designs stacked along two resort coasts. But some of the best rounds here are the cheap ones — oceanfront municipals and upcountry layouts where the trade winds do the defending. ## West Maui: Kapalua & Ka'anapali

**[The Plantation Course at Kapalua](/courses/maui-004-b/)** — Resort · Par 73, 7,411 yds · $475 visitors ($445 resort guests)
The island's crown jewel and host of The Sentry, the PGA Tour's season-opening event each January. The Coore/Crenshaw design tumbles across plantation slopes with ocean views from nearly every hole. Caddies available ($160–185); premium club rental $95. Book well ahead — and check conditions, because the Kapalua wind is legendary.

**[The Bay Course at Kapalua](/courses/maui-004-a/)** — Resort · Temporarily closed
The classic Arnold Palmer/Francis Duane design is not currently operating following its renovation work. The Plantation Course is Kapalua's only tee time right now — check the [Bay Course page](/courses/maui-004-a/) for reopening news.

**[Royal Ka'anapali Course](/courses/maui-002-a/)** — Resort · Par 71, 6,700 yds · Dynamic pricing
Robert Trent Jones Sr.'s North Course at Ka'anapali Resort — the more demanding of the two Ka'anapali layouts, with a storied tournament history. The "hana hou" same-day replay is just $69; club rental $75.

**[Ka'anapali Kai Course](/courses/maui-002-b/)** — Resort · Par 70, 6,400 yds · Dynamic pricing
The friendlier Arthur Jack Snyder design — shorter, tighter, and ideal for a second round of the day (replay $49) or mixed-skill groups.

## South Maui: Wailea, Makena & Kihei

**[Gold Course at Wailea](/courses/maui-011-a/)** — Resort · Par 72, 7,078 yds · $325; $225 after 12 PM
Robert Trent Jones Jr.'s strategic masterpiece, former host of the LPGA Skins Game. Rugged lava-rock framing, ocean views, and serious teeth. Afternoon rate saves $100.

**[Emerald Course at Wailea](/courses/maui-011-b/)** — Resort · Par 70, 6,100 yds · $325; $225 after 12 PM
The more forgiving Wailea option — same price as the Gold, friendlier landing areas, arguably prettier flower-lined corridors.

**[Old Blue Course at Wailea](/courses/maui-011-c/)** — Resort · Par 71, 6,300 yds · $265; $175 after 12 PM
Wailea's original layout (1972) and its best value — a relaxed, classic resort round at $60 less than its siblings.

**[Makena Golf Course](/courses/maui-006/)** — Members only · Par 72, 7,038 yds
Important change for trip planners: the former resort course south of Wailea is now **private, members-only** (guests with a member, ~$250–300). Don't build an itinerary around it unless you have a connection.

**[Maui Nui Golf Club](/courses/maui-008/)** — Public · Par 71, 6,801 yds · $99–$109; $64–$74 after 2 PM
South Maui's value anchor in Kihei — West Maui Mountain views, honest conditions, and the island's best late-day price. Juniors play free after 3 PM with a paying adult.

## Central Maui: Kahului & Wailuku

**[The Dunes at Maui Lani](/courses/maui-001/)** — Public · Par 72, 6,841 yds · $129 morning; $89 after 11 AM; $69 after 2 PM
Scottish-inspired links with real dunes, elevation change, and Haleakala views — minutes from the airport, so it's the classic "first round off the plane." Afternoon pricing is outstanding.

**[Waiehu Golf Course](/courses/maui-010/)** — Municipal · Par 72, 6,330 yds · $66–$86 non-resident
Maui's county muni and one of Hawaii's best bargains — oceanfront holes on Maui's north coast and a former USGA Amateur Public Links host. Twilight drops as low as $33–$43 for visitors.

**[Kahili Golf Course](/courses/maui-003/)** — Closed
The elevated Wailuku course with mountain views is **currently closed**. Check the [course page](/courses/maui-003/) for status changes before counting it into any plan.

**[King Kamehameha Golf Club](/courses/maui-005/)** — Private · Par 72, 6,800 yds
Members-only club in the West Maui Mountain foothills with a famous Frank Lloyd Wright-designed clubhouse. Visitor access requires a member.

## Upcountry & North Shore

**[Pukalani Country Club](/courses/maui-009/)** — Public/Semi-private · Par 72, 6,882 yds · $109 ($89 from May 1)
Upcountry's cooler air and panoramic bi-coastal views, 1,100 feet up the Haleakala slope. The super-twilight rate after 1 PM is just **$49** — possibly the best golf value on Maui.

**[Maui Country Club](/courses/maui-007/)** — Private · 9 holes, Par 36
Historic members' club near Paia on the North Shore, a short walk from Baldwin Beach.

## Practical Tips for Playing Maui

1. **Book Kapalua and Wailea well ahead.** The Sentry's home course sells out in peak season; Wailea's afternoon slots ($225 vs $325) go first.
2. **Afternoon trades are real.** Wind builds after lunch, especially at Kapalua and the north-facing courses — morning tee times are calmer, afternoon rounds are cheaper. Pick your trade-off.
3. **Stack the twilight deals.** Pukalani $49 after 1 PM, Dunes $69 after 2 PM, Maui Nui from $64 after 2 PM, Wailea $225 after noon — Maui rewards the late tee time.
4. **Humidity eats gloves.** Rotate two or three — the [FootJoy WeatherSof](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0CCK9CRGX?tag=hawaiigolfg02-20) handles it well — and keep a spare dry.
5. **Do the rental math.** Premium sets cost $75 at Ka'anapali and $105 at Wailea — over four-plus rounds, that exceeds airline bag fees. Frequent visitors sometimes ship clubs ahead with [Ship Sticks](https://www.shipsticks.com) or grab a pre-owned set from [2nd Swing](https://www.2ndswing.com). Our [Hawaii golf packing list](/blog/hawaii-golf-packing-list/) has the full breakdown.
6. **Kama'aina: bring ID.** Resident rates are deep — Waiehu from $17, Maui Nui $34–$59, Pukalani $35–$49, Wailea from $90–$150 by time of day.
7. **Plan around the closures.** With Kahili closed and Kapalua's Bay Course temporarily closed, West Maui tee sheets are tighter than the brochures suggest — reserve early.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many golf courses does Maui have?**
Maui has 15 golf courses: ten open to visitors (Kapalua Plantation, two at Ka'anapali, three at Wailea, the Dunes at Maui Lani, Maui Nui, Pukalani, and Waiehu), two private clubs, one members-only former resort (Makena), and two currently closed (Kahili and Kapalua's Bay Course).

**What is the best golf course on Maui?**
The Plantation Course at Kapalua is the consensus pick — a top-100 caliber Coore/Crenshaw design and host of the PGA Tour's Sentry every January. For pure playability on a resort budget, Wailea's Old Blue ($265) is the local insider's answer.

**Is the Bay Course at Kapalua open?**
Not currently — the Bay Course is temporarily closed, leaving the Plantation Course as Kapalua's only operating layout. Kahili Golf Course in Wailuku is also closed. Both course pages on our site track status changes.

**How much does it cost to golf on Maui?**
Visitor green fees range from $49 (Pukalani super twilight) and $66 (Waiehu weekday) up to $475 at Kapalua's Plantation Course. Most Maui resort golf falls between $225 and $325, with afternoon twilight rates cutting 25–35% at nearly every course.

**What is the cheapest golf course on Maui?**
Waiehu Golf Course, the county muni, at $66–$86 for visitors (twilight as low as $33) — with genuine oceanfront holes. Pukalani's $49 super twilight and Maui Nui's $64 afternoon rate round out the value podium.

**When is the best time of day to play Maui?**
Mornings bring lighter wind and quicker greens; afternoons bring 25–40% twilight discounts and stronger trades. In winter (December–March), morning rounds also dodge the day's passing showers, and you might spot humpback whales breaching offshore between shots.
