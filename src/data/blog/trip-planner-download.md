## 🏝️ Island Selection Guide

### Oahu — 37 Courses
- **Best for:** Variety and accessibility
- **Top picks:** Turtle Bay, Ko Olina, Kapolei Golf Club
- **Budget-friendly:** Ala Wai, Navy-Marine GC
- **When to play:** Year-round, drier on the leeward side

### Maui — 15 Courses
- **Best for:** Resort golf experiences
- **Top picks:** Kapalua Plantation, Wailea Blue, King Kamehameha
- **Budget-friendly:** Maui Nui, Waiehu Municipal
- **When to play:** Morning tee times recommended (less wind)

### Kauai — 8 Courses
- **Best for:** Scenic beauty
- **Top picks:** Princeville Makai, Poipu Bay, Ocean Course at Hokuala
- **Budget-friendly:** Kukuiolono GC ($15 all day!)
- **When to play:** Avoid heavy rain season (Nov-Mar)

### Big Island — 19 Courses
- **Best for:** Dramatic landscapes
- **Top picks:** Mauna Kea, Hualalai, Waikoloa
- **Budget-friendly:** Hilo Municipal, Volcano GC
- **When to play:** Kona side is drier; Hilo is lush but wetter

### Lanai — 3 Courses
- **Best for:** Exclusive luxury
- **Top picks:** Manele Golf Course (Four Seasons)
- **Note:** Limited options, all high-end

### Molokai — 1 Course
- **Best for:** Off-the-beaten-path experience
- **Top pick:** Ironwood Hills Golf Course
- **Note:** Rustic, historic, very affordable

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Book early** — Popular courses fill up 30-90 days in advance
2. **Twilight rates** — Save 30-50% by booking after 1-2 PM
3. **Kama'aina rates** — Hawaii residents get discounts (bring HI ID)
4. **Military rates** — Active duty and retirees save significantly
5. **Club rental** — Most courses offer rentals ($35-80); consider shipping your own for longer trips
6. **Weather** — Morning is usually calmer and drier; afternoons can be windy
7. **Hydration** — Bring extra water; Hawaii heat + humidity is intense
8. **Respect the land** — Stay on cart paths, repair divots, rake bunkers
