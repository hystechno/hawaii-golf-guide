## Quick Answers

**How many golf courses are in Hawaii?**  
Hawaii has over 80 golf courses across six islands, with the majority located on Oahu (37 courses), Maui (20+ courses), and the Big Island (20+ courses).

**What is the best island for golf in Hawaii?**  
Maui and the Big Island offer the best combination of championship courses, scenic views, and resort amenities. Oahu has the most variety and accessibility.

**How much does it cost to golf in Hawaii?**  
Green fees range from $30-$50 at municipal courses to $200-$400 at premium resort courses. The average cost is $100-$150 per round.

## Introduction

Hawaii is a golfer's paradise. With year-round tropical weather, stunning ocean views, and world-class courses designed by legends like Jack Nicklaus, Arnold Palmer, and Robert Trent Jones Jr., the Hawaiian Islands offer some of the most spectacular golfing experiences on Earth.

Whether you're a scratch golfer looking to play championship layouts or a casual player seeking affordable municipal courses with ocean views, Hawaii has something for everyone. This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to know about golfing in Hawaii - from the best islands and courses to costs, tips, and must-know information for planning your perfect Hawaii golf vacation.

## Best Islands for Golf in Hawaii

### 1. Maui - The Golf Capital of Hawaii

Maui consistently ranks as the best Hawaiian island for golf, and for good reason. The island is home to the famous [Plantation Course at Kapalua](../courses/plantation-course-kapalua) - host of the PGA Tour's season-opening Tournament of Champions - and the stunning [Wailea Golf Club](../courses/wailea-golf-club) with its three championship courses.

**Why Maui is great for golf:**
- **Championship courses:** Play where the pros play
- **Resort amenities:** World-class hotels and spas adjacent to courses
- **Perfect weather:** Consistent 75-85°F year-round
- **Scenic variety:** Ocean views, mountain backdrops, and volcanic landscapes

**Top Maui Courses:**
- [Plantation Course at Kapalua](../courses/plantation-course-kapalua) - $295-$395
- [Wailea Gold Course](../courses/wailea-gold-course) - $185-$275
- [Wailea Emerald Course](../courses/wailea-emerald-course) - $165-$245
- [Wailea Blue Course](../courses/wailea-blue-course) - $145-$225

### 2. Big Island - Dramatic Landscapes

The Big Island offers the most dramatic golfing backdrop in Hawaii, with courses set against active volcanoes, black lava fields, and ocean cliffs. The [Hualalai Golf Course](../courses/hualalai-golf-course) at Four Seasons and [Mauna Kea Golf Course](../courses/mauna-kea-golf-course) are bucket-list courses for serious golfers.

**Why the Big Island excels:**
- **Unique terrain:** Play on lava rock and volcanic soil
- **Luxury resorts:** Four Seasons, Fairmont, and Mauna Kea Beach Hotel
- **Less crowded:** More tee time availability than Maui or Oahu
- **Dramatic elevation changes:** Challenging and memorable holes

**Top Big Island Courses:**
- [Hualalai Golf Course](../courses/hualalai-golf-course) - $295-$395 (resort guests only)
- [Mauna Kea Golf Course](../courses/mauna-kea-golf-course) - $225-$325
- [Mauna Lani North Course](../courses/mauna-lani-north-course) - $185-$275
- [Mauna Lani South Course](../courses/mauna-lani-south-course) - $175-$265

### 3. Oahu - Most Accessible & Affordable

Oahu offers the most golf courses (37) and the best mix of affordable municipal tracks and high-end resort courses. It's also the easiest island to reach with the most flight options into Honolulu.

**Why Oahu is ideal:**
- **Most courses:** 37 options across the island
- **Best value:** Municipal courses from $30-$75
- **Convenience:** Fly direct to Honolulu from most US cities
- **Variety:** Everything from beachside links to mountain courses

**Top Oahu Courses:**
- [Turtle Bay Palmer Course](../courses/turtle-bay-palmer-course) - $175-$250
- [Ko Olina Golf Club](../courses/ko-olina-golf-club) - $185-$275
- [Ted Makalena Golf Course](../courses/ted-makalena-golf-course) - $50-$85 (municipal)
- [Ala Wai Golf Course](../courses/ala-wai-golf-course) - $42-$75 (municipal, Waikiki)

### 4. Kauai - Natural Beauty

Kauai is the least developed Hawaiian island and offers a more natural, less commercialized golf experience. The [Princeville Makai Golf Club](../courses/princeville-makai-golf-club) is consistently ranked among the most scenic courses in the world.

**Top Kauai Courses:**
- [Princeville Makai Golf Club](../courses/princeville-makai-golf-club) - $225-$375
- [Poipu Bay Golf Course](../courses/poipu-bay-golf-course) - $165-$245
- [Puakea Golf Course](../courses/puakea-golf-course) - $95-$145

### 5. Lanai - Exclusive Luxury

Lanai offers two spectacular Four Seasons courses - the [Manele Golf Course](../courses/manele-golf-course) (oceanfront) and [Cavendish Golf Course](../courses/cavendish-golf-course). Access is limited as you must ferry or fly from Maui.

### 6. Molokai - Off the Beaten Path

Molokai has one 9-hole course - [Ironwood Hills Golf Club](../courses/ironwood-hills-golf-club) - offering an authentic local experience with minimal tourism.

## Golf Course Costs in Hawaii

### Green Fee Breakdown by Category

| Course Type | Price Range | Examples |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| **Municipal Courses** | $30 - $85 | Ala Wai, Ted Makalena, Pali |
| **Daily Fee Courses** | $75 - $150 | Hawaii Prince, Coral Creek, Pearl Country Club |
| **Resort Courses** | $150 - $275 | Wailea, Mauna Lani, Ko Olina |
| **Premium/Championship** | $275 - $400 | Kapalua Plantation, Princeville, Hualalai |

### Additional Costs to Budget For

- **Club rentals:** $50-$75 per round (bring your own if possible)
- **Caddies:** $50-$100 + tip (premium courses)
- **Golf cart:** Usually included in green fee
- **Range balls:** $10-$20 per bucket
- **Tips:** $5-$10 for bag attendants

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Play twilight rounds** - Save 30-50% after 2 PM
2. **Book through golf packagers** - HawaiiGolfDeals.com, GolfNow
3. **Stay-and-play packages** - Often include unlimited golf
4. **Municipal courses** - Great value on Oahu and Maui
5. **Kama'aina rates** - Hawaii residents save 20-40%

## When to Golf in Hawaii

### Best Time to Visit

**Peak Season (December - March):**
- Pros: Perfect weather, whale watching
- Cons: Highest prices, crowded tee sheets
- Green fees: Premium pricing (+20-40%)

**Shoulder Season (April - May, September - November):**
- Pros: Good weather, lower prices, less crowded
- Cons: Occasional rain on windward sides
- Green fees: Standard pricing

**Low Season (June - August):**
- Pros: Lowest prices, available tee times
- Cons: Hotter temperatures (85-90°F), hurricane risk (rare)
- Green fees: Discounted (-10-20%)

### Weather Considerations

- **Trade winds:** Can be strong (15-25 mph) - adjust club selection
- **Morning vs. afternoon:** Morning typically calmer
- **Rain:** Brief showers common on windward (east/north) sides
- **Temperature:** 70-85°F year-round at sea level

## Essential Tips for Golfing in Hawaii

### What to Pack

**Clothing:**
- Light, breathable fabrics (cotton, moisture-wicking)
- Collared shirts required at most courses
- Golf shorts or pants (jeans not permitted)
- Windbreaker or light jacket (trade winds)
- Rain gear (compact umbrella or rain jacket)

**Equipment:**
- Extra golf balls (wind will cost you balls!)
- Sunscreen (reef-safe required in Hawaii)
- Sunglasses
- Hat or visor
- Bug spray (especially for twilight rounds)

**Important:** Check baggage fees - most airlines charge $30-$50 each way for golf clubs.

### Course Etiquette in Hawaii

- **Repair divots and ball marks** - Courses take pride in conditioning
- **Rake bunkers** - Leave it better than you found it
- **Dress code** - Collared shirts required at most courses
- **Pace of play** - 4 hours is standard; faster during peak season
- **Cell phones** - Silence on course; okay in carts

### Playing in Wind

Hawaiian trade winds are no joke - 15-25 mph is common, with gusts to 40 mph on exposed holes.

**Wind Tips:**
- Club up 1-2 clubs into the wind
- Club down 1-2 clubs downwind
- Aim for wider parts of greens
- Putts break more in wind
- Use knockdown shots to keep ball under wind

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need a rental car to golf in Hawaii?

**Oahu:** Yes, unless staying in Waikiki and playing only Ala Wai (accessible by bus/taxi).  
**Maui:** Yes - courses spread across island.  
**Big Island:** Definitely yes - courses 30-60 minutes apart.  
**Kauai:** Yes - limited public transit.  
**Lanai:** Golf shuttles available from Four Seasons.

### Can I rent clubs in Hawaii?

Yes, all courses offer club rentals ($50-$75/day). Quality varies - premium courses have newer sets. Consider bringing your own if you play multiple rounds (airline fees may be less than rental costs).

### What's the dress code for Hawaiian golf courses?

Standard golf attire required:
- Collared shirts (polo or button-down)
- Golf shorts or pants (no denim)
- Golf shoes or athletic shoes
- Some courses prohibit metal spikes

### Are Hawaii golf courses walker-friendly?

Most courses require carts due to:
- Heat and humidity
- Elevation changes
- Distance between holes
- Pace of play requirements

Walking typically allowed only at twilight or by special request.

### Do Hawaii courses offer lessons?

Yes, most resort and daily-fee courses have PGA professionals offering:
- Individual lessons: $75-$150/hour
- Group clinics: $30-$50/person
- Playing lessons: $200-$400 (includes green fee)

Book in advance, especially during peak season.

### What's the best course for beginners in Hawaii?

**Top beginner-friendly courses:**
- Wailea Blue Course (Maui) - shorter, forgiving
- Mauna Lani North (Big Island) - wide fairways
- Ko Olina (Oahu) - resort amenities
- Pali Golf Course (Oahu) - affordable, municipal

### Can I play Hawaiian courses in the rain?

Yes - light rain is common and play continues. Courses only close for:
- Lightning
- Heavy flooding
- Hurricane warnings (rare)

Bring rain gear and embrace the tropical experience!
